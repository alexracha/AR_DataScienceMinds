# Brief

## ICP
- Owner/COO/ops lead at a 10–500 person service, trades, healthcare-admin, or professional-services firm.
- Trigger: already bought AI tools, but nothing runs a real process; or pressure from competitors/staff.

## Pains (ranked, buyer language)
1. "We bought it" but it doesn't "run the process" (tools bought, little production use).
2. No clear plan; "three overlapping subscriptions".
3. Team is "winging it"; needs training.
4. Unsure AI applies to their business.
5. Can't tell AI spend from AI results.

## Objections (ranked)
1. Data security/privacy → plain-language data handling, NDA before access, no training on client data, work in their tools.
2. AI accuracy/trust → human-in-the-loop by default, pilot before scale.
3. Cost/ROI → free audit with written deliverable; prioritize by payback; fixed-scope pricing.
4. "Not relevant to us" → audit starts from their workflows, not from tools.
5. Failed pilot fear → 2–4 week proof, defined success metric, stop if it doesn't pay back.

## Positioning
For owners of 10–500 person businesses who have bought AI tools but see no results, AR DataScienceMinds finds the two or three workflows where AI pays back fastest, builds them, and trains your team to run them — unlike tool vendors and generic agencies, because we start from your process and measure payback before we scale.

## Mechanism: the "Find · Prove · Scale" method
1. **Find** — map workflows, rank by payback.
2. **Prove** — build one pilot in 2–4 weeks against one metric.
3. **Scale** — roll out, train the team, hand over.

## Offer, CTA & form
- Primary CTA: "Get your free AI opportunity audit".
- Visitor gets: a 30-minute call and a written summary of top opportunities. (Timing "within 2 business days" is a commitment for the owner to confirm.)
- After submit: reply within 1 business day, book the call.
- Form: name, work email, company (required); "What would you like AI to fix?" (required, short). Optional: team size, budget range. Consent line + privacy link.
