# Research: AI Consulting Landing Page

Method note: WebSearch worked; WebFetch was blocked by the network egress proxy (mannventure.com, firstmovers.ai, landingi.com). Findings below come from search-result summaries only. Competitor teardown is therefore thin, and figures are single-source unless noted.

## 1. Buyer pains & motives
- Owners struggle to know which AI tools fit their workflow, not which have the best marketing. Consultants filter tools against the firm's own systems and data. Source: [HyScaler](https://hyscaler.com/insights/ai-consulting-for-small-business/) (search summary)
- The gap between profiting from AI and merely spending on it is usually having a clear plan, not budget or skill. Source: [Dan Cumberland Labs](https://dancumberlandlabs.com/blog/ai-consulting-small-business/) (search summary)
- 70% of owners say they need more training to use AI properly (Thryv, Apr 2026). Source: [Atlas Unchained](https://www.atlasunchained.com/ai-for-local-business/ai-training-gap-one-week-fix/) (search summary)
- 82% of small employers have invested in AI tools, yet production use is under 20% (SBE Council 2026). Source: [Atlas Unchained](https://www.atlasunchained.com/ai-for-local-business/ai-training-gap-one-week-fix/) (search summary; unverified at primary source)
- 80% of organizations report no meaningful business impact from AI investment. Source: [Atlas Unchained](https://www.atlasunchained.com/ai-for-local-business/ai-training-gap-one-week-fix/) (unverified, single source)
- 74% of small business owners are using or testing AI; 68% think AI will be good for their business (Bluevine, Apr 2026, n=942). Source: [The Small Business Expo](https://www.thesmallbusinessexpo.com/blog/the-trust-gap-small-businesses-are-using-ai-more-but-still-dont-fully-trust-it/)

## 2. Objections & fears
- 82% of SMBs report at least one barrier to deeper AI use: data security 33% (up from 23%), distrust of accuracy 31%. Source: [Small Business Expo](https://www.thesmallbusinessexpo.com/blog/the-trust-gap-small-businesses-are-using-ai-more-but-still-dont-fully-trust-it/)
- 78% of SMB owners don't fully trust AI on low-level tasks without oversight. Source: [KVIA/Stacker](https://kvia.com/stacker-small-business/2026/07/16/3-4-of-small-businesses-dont-trust-ai-for-basic-tasks/)
- 38% of non-adopters cite data privacy as a barrier. Source: same as above
- Relevance doubt: ~82% of firms under 5 employees not planning AI cited relevance. Source: [SBA Advocacy](https://advocacy.sba.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Research-Spotlight-AI-in-Business-Small-Firms-Closing-In_-092425.pdf) (search summary)
- Cost/ROI doubt implied by wide price ranges (below); "bought tools nobody uses" is a recurring theme.

## 5. Offer & pricing models
- Focused SMB AI assessment/audit: about $2,000–$10,000; audit/strategy overall $1,500–$15,000. Source: [Innovative Compass](https://www.innovativecompass.com/blog-how-much-does-ai-consulting-cost), [Bosio Digital](https://bosio.digital/articles/ai-consulting-cost-guide) (search summaries)
- Small-business AI audits $2,500–$15,000; readiness assessments $3K–$25K. Source: [ConsultKit](https://www.consultkit.ai/blog/how-to-price-an-ai-audit-for-a-small-business-with-real-numbers-1774170452753)
- Retainers for small business $2,000–$8,000/month. Source: [AI Smart Ventures](https://aismartventures.com/posts/ai-consulting-costs-for-small-business-what-owner-operators-actually-pay-in-2026/)
- Typical 4–6 week implementation: $10K–$15K including integration and training. Source: [Leanware](https://leanware.co/insights/how-much-does-an-ai-consultant-cost) (search summary)
- Hourly: independents $150–$350; boutiques $250–$500. Source: [AI Essentials](https://aiessentials.us/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-hire-an-ai-consultant-for-my-small)
- Trend: value-based pricing at 10–40% of attributable savings/revenue. Source: [Leanware](https://leanware.co/insights/how-much-does-an-ai-consultant-cost) (single source)
- Free audit call plus deliverable is a live market pattern (MannVenture).

## 6. Trust signals
- Named testimonials, industry credentials, short case summaries carry more weight for consulting offers. Source: [Landingi](https://landingi.com/landing-page/41-best-practices/) (search summary)
- Place the strongest proof next to the claim it supports. Same source.
- Clear data-security statements reduce form abandonment. Same source.
- Case-study metrics seen in market (illustrative, not ours to reuse): ~15 h/week saved on admin; 40h → 8h/week on logistics processing; deployments live in 2–4 weeks. Source: [Cyndra](https://www.cyndra.ai/blog/ai-business-automation-case-studies), [Octavius](https://octavius.ai/business-automation/ai-automation-case-studies-small-business/) (search summaries; vendor-reported, unverified)
